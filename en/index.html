1. Smachna Molochka — family-run cheesemaker

Challenge: the brand lacked a cohesive visual identity and a clear distinction from a larger competitor. Inconsistent logos and designs across formats weakened recognition and made it harder to build a lasting brand image.

  • Analysed local competitors and the category’s visual landscape
  • Defined the brand territory: family, craft, natural ingredients and the family’s own farm
  • Developed the logo, brand graphics, colour and typography system
  • Applied the identity to packaging, labels and advertising materials, accounting for print requirements
  • Designed advertising creatives for social media and local targeting, social media templates and guidelines

Results:

  • Sales increased by 20% in the two months after launch; prices rose by 10% without a drop in volume
  • Advertising CTR was 2.1%
  • Social media engagement increased from 2% to 4%
  • The brand added one new point of sale

3 months

2. PLIE — ballet school, Portugal

Challenge: the students are children, but parents make the purchasing decision. An overly childlike style undermined trust in the school’s professionalism, while an overly formal one did not appeal to children. The school was losing trial-class enquiries and could not launch adult classes.

  • Analysed the audience, competitors and the visual landscape of ballet schools
  • Defined a brand territory combining childlike lightness with the aesthetic of classical ballet
  • Developed a logo with adaptable versions of the mark, a colour and typography system
  • Created original patterns inspired by Giselle, Swan Lake and The Nutcracker — a recognisable brand asset even without the logo
  • Designed a responsive website with a timetable and class descriptions
  • Created social media advertising assets, social media templates and brand guidelines

Results:

  • Instagram advertising CTR was 1.5%
  • Instagram engagement was 4.5%
  • The website converted 4% of visits into enquiries
  • 32 new students joined in the first season, and an adult class opened

2 months

3. RINREI — dietary supplement brand, Kazan

Challenge: the new brand wanted to move away from the familiar pharmacy aesthetic and present supplements as part of a healthy lifestyle, rather than as ‘pills’. The client also ruled out standalone brand graphics.

  • Analysed the category’s visual codes and identified an unoccupied territory away from pharmaceutical aesthetics
  • Built the concept around balance, nutrition, movement and self-care
  • Developed the logo, colour and typography system
  • Built brand recognition through typography and metaphor-led compositions
  • Defined composition principles for product and advertising communications, and created social media templates and guidelines

Results:

  • Prepared the brand’s visual system for a full marketplace launch in Q4 2026

1 month

4. 5 STARS — clothing brand

Challenge: the brand lacked a visual language that captured the feel of 2000s America and could evolve consistently across clothing collections and communications.

  • Defined the visual direction using recognisable American imagery and cultural references
  • Designed the logo and its main variations for different formats
  • Developed an extended colour palette and typography system suitable for apparel
  • Developed composition principles and rules for applying the identity in digital communications
  • Contributed to the visual design of the first clothing collection
  • Documented how to use the system in the brand guidelines

Results:

  • Developed the visual identity from scratch in one month
  • Adapted the system for two key applications: digital communications and apparel
  • Applied the visual system to five pieces in the first collection
